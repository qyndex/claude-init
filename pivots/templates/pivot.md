## Pivot type (Lean Startup taxonomy)

Pick one. The taxonomy distinguishes degree-of-change:

- `zoom-in` — one feature of the product becomes the entire product
- `zoom-out` — the product becomes one feature of a larger product
- `customer-segment` — same product, different customers
- `customer-need` — same customers, different need addressed
- `platform` — application ↔ platform shift
- `business-architecture` — B2B ↔ B2C, high-margin ↔ volume
- `value-capture` — monetization model change
- `engine-of-growth` — viral ↔ paid ↔ sticky retention loop
- `channel` — direct sales ↔ self-serve ↔ partners
- `technology` — same problem, fundamentally different tech approach
- `tactical-correction` — small adjustment, not a true pivot (most common in healthy teams)
